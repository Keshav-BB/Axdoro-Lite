# Shipping & Delivery Policy – AXDORO Clothing

*Last Updated: September 2026*

At **AXDORO**, we ensure your 240 GSM heavyweight apparel reaches you promptly and safely via reliable logistics partners.

---

## 1. Processing Time
- All verified orders are processed, packaged, and dispatched from our central warehouse within **24 to 48 business hours** (Monday through Saturday, excluding national holidays).
- Orders placed on Sundays or public holidays will be dispatched on the next operational business day.

---

## 2. Shipping Charges
- **Orders Above ₹999**: FREE Pan-India Standard Shipping.
- **Orders Below ₹999**: A nominal flat shipping charge of ₹70 is applied at checkout.

---

## 3. Estimated Delivery Timelines
Once dispatched, parcels typically arrive within the following windows:
- **Tier 1 Metros (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata)**: 3 to 5 business days.
- **Tier 2 Cities and State Capitals**: 4 to 6 business days.
- **Rest of India (Remote and North-East regions)**: 5 to 8 business days.

*(Please note: Inclement weather, festival peaks, or regional transport restrictions may occasionally cause minor delays).*

---

## 4. Order Tracking
Once your package is handed over to the courier:
- You will receive an AWB tracking number via WhatsApp and/or Email.
- You can track live courier milestones anytime by visiting our [Track Order](https://axdoro.com/track-order/) page using your Order ID and phone number.

---

## 5. Damaged or Tampered Parcels
If the outer packaging appears visibly damaged, torn, or tampered with at the time of delivery, please do not accept the parcel from the delivery agent and capture clear photos. Contact our WhatsApp support team immediately at **+91 88073 04713** so we can coordinate a swift replacement.
