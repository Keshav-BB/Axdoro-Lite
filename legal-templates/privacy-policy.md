# Privacy Policy – AXDORO Clothing

*Last Updated: September 2026*

Welcome to **AXDORO** (accessible at [axdoro.com](https://axdoro.com)). We respect your personal privacy and are committed to protecting the information you share with us. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, handle, and safeguard customer data in compliance with applicable Indian data protection guidelines.

---

## 1. Information We Collect
When you browse our store, create an account, or place an order, we may collect:
- **Contact Information**: Full Name, WhatsApp / Mobile phone number, and Email address.
- **Delivery Information**: Complete shipping address, City, State, and PIN code.
- **Transaction Identifiers**: WooCommerce Order ID, Payment Gateway reference/transaction ID, or customer-submitted Bank Reference Number / UTR. (Note: AXDORO does not directly collect or store credit card numbers, UPI PINs, or banking passwords; payments are securely handled through regulated payment gateways or UPI networks).
- **Technical Information**: IP address, browser type, and cookie identifiers for session management and bag persistence.

---

## 2. Purpose of Data Collection
We process your personal information strictly for legitimate commercial purposes:
- Fulfilling orders, preparing shipments, and managing deliveries.
- Communicating order statuses, payment links, and AWB tracking numbers via WhatsApp, SMS, or Email.
- Responding to size consultations, exchange requests, and customer service inquiries.
- Preventing fraud, duplicate transactions, and unauthorized account access.

---

## 3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Processors
We do not sell, rent, or lease customer data. We share necessary operational data only with trusted partners essential to delivering your order:
- **Logistics Providers**: Shiprocket and assigned courier partners (e.g., Delhivery, BlueDart) to execute doorstep parcel deliveries.
- **Payment Providers**: Razorpay, Cashfree, or NPCI UPI networks for payment verification and settlement.
- **Communication Services**: Authorized WhatsApp Business API providers for transactional milestone alerts.

---

## 4. Cookies and Session Storage
We use functional cookies to remember items in your Bag, maintain customer login state, and optimize mobile browsing performance. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, though doing so may restrict certain e-commerce functions.

---

## 5. Data Security & Storage
We employ industry-standard SSL (Secure Socket Layer) encryption and strict WordPress access controls. Only authorized shop managers have access to customer shipping records.
