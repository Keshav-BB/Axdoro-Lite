# Return, Exchange & Refund Policy – AXDORO Clothing

*Last Updated: September 2026*

At **AXDORO**, customer satisfaction is at the core of what we build. We want you to feel confident in the fit, weight, and silhouette of your 240 GSM garments.

---

## 1. 7-Day Size Exchange Window
- We offer a **7-Day Hassle-Free Exchange Policy** for sizing adjustments from the date your parcel is marked as delivered by the courier.
- If your oversized tee feels too loose or too fitted, we will gladly exchange it for another size in the same style, subject to stock availability.

---

## 2. Exchange Eligibility Criteria
To be eligible for an exchange:
- The garment must be unworn, unwashed, and free from perfume, stains, or deodorant marks.
- Original brand tags, barcode labels, and poly-bag packaging must be intact.
- A valid AXDORO Order ID (e.g., `#1024`) must be provided.

---

## 3. How to Initiate an Exchange
1. Message our WhatsApp Support Team at **+91 88073 04713** within 7 days of delivery.
2. State your Order ID and the preferred replacement size.
3. Our team will arrange a reverse pickup from your shipping address via Shiprocket reverse logistics.
4. Once the returned item passes quality inspection at our warehouse, your replacement size will be dispatched immediately.

---

## 4. Defective or Incorrect Items
Every AXDORO garment undergoes rigorous quality checks before dispatch. In the rare event you receive a manufacturing defect or the wrong item:
- Notify us on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery with clear photographs of the defect.
- We will arrange an immediate reverse pickup and ship a brand new replacement at zero additional cost to you.

---

## 5. Refunds & Cancellations
- **Order Cancellation**: You may cancel an order before it has been dispatched by messaging our WhatsApp support desk. A full refund will be initiated to your original payment method.
- **Refund Processing**: In cases where an exchange cannot be fulfilled due to inventory exhaustion, a full refund will be processed within 5–7 working days to the customer's source account (UPI / Bank Account).
- **Custom / Bulk Orders**: Customized merchandise (custom prints, team embroidery) is tailored specifically to client specifications and is non-returnable unless defective.
