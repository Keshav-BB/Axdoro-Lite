THE AXDORO MANIFESTO

ENGINEERED FOR SUBSTANCE

AXDORO was born from a frustration with standard menswear in India. Most t-shirts available on the market weigh between 160 and 180 GSM. While cheap to produce, they cling uncomfortably, turn transparent in light hues, and lose their neckline structure within a handful of washes.

We made the deliberate choice to build our foundation around 240 GSM super combed cotton. The increased yarn count delivers an architectural drape that holds its shape independently, accentuating broader shoulders and a natural drop silhouette.
