BRAND ORIGINS

THE AXDORO STORY

Redefining Indian streetwear through structured fabrics and uncompromising fit.

BORN FOR THE STREETS

AXDORO began with a simple observation: everyday apparel in India was either flimsy and shapeless or locked behind inflated designer markups. We set out to bridge this divide by engineering everyday streetwear staples that deliver high-density texture, structured silhouettes, and accessible pricing.

We believe great style starts with the weight and hand-feel of the fabric. Rather than chasing fleeting micro-trends, our collections prioritize timeless drop-shoulder fits, versatile earth tones, and enduring craftsmanship.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pay using UPI via WhatsApp? ▼

When you place an order on our store, a unique WooCommerce Order ID is generated first. You can then complete payment directly on WhatsApp using GPay, PhonePe, or Paytm, and submit your 12-digit UTR reference right on the order screen.

What makes 240 GSM tees different? ▼

240 GSM indicates high fabric weight and density. Unlike flimsy 160 GSM shirts, our tees hold an authentic boxy drape, eliminate transparency, and do not lose their collar shape after washing.

How do I track my shipment? ▼

You can visit our Track Order page and enter your Order ID & phone number to view live courier status milestones.

What is your exchange policy? ▼

We offer a 7-day hassle-free size exchange from the date of delivery. Garments must be unworn with original tags attached. Simply message us on WhatsApp with your Order ID!
