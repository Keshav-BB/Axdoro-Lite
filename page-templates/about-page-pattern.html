THE AXDORO STORY

Redefining Indian streetwear through structured fabrics and uncompromising fit.

BORN FOR THE STREETS

AXDORO began with a simple observation: everyday apparel in India was either flimsy and shapeless or locked behind inflated designer markups. We set out to bridge this divide by engineering everyday streetwear staples that deliver high-density texture, structured silhouettes, and accessible pricing.

We believe great style starts with the weight and hand-feel of the fabric. Rather than chasing fleeting micro-trends, our collections prioritize timeless drop-shoulder fits, versatile earth tones, and enduring craftsmanship.

THE 240 GSM STANDARD

GSM stands for Grams per Square Meter — the measure of fabric density and substance.

01. HEAVYWEIGHT STRUCTURE

At 240 GSM, our cotton achieves an architectural drape. The garment holds its own shape independently, creating that coveted boxy streetwear aesthetic without feeling rigid.

02. COMPLETE OPACITY

No see-through silhouettes. Whether you choose Crisp Off-White or Deep Black, our dense weave delivers complete coverage and a substantial physical presence.

03. REINFORCED COLLARS

Every AXDORO crewneck features a heavy 1.25-inch elastane-ribbed collar double-stitched to maintain a clean neckline wash after wash.

OUR ROADMAP

From 240 GSM heavy tees to complete contemporary wardrobe staples.

PHASE 1: THE FOUNDATION

Perfecting the Oversized and Half-Sleeve heavy tee in core streetwear tones. Expanding size availability from XS to XXL.

PHASE 2: STREETWEAR LAYERING

Introducing heavyweight hoodies (380+ GSM), boxy overshirts, cargo sweatpants, and signature graphic capsules.
