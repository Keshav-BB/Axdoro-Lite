FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How do I pay using UPI on WhatsApp?

When you checkout on the AXDORO website, your order is generated first with a unique Order ID. You will be provided a direct button to pay via WhatsApp and a UPI link compatible with GPay, PhonePe, and Paytm. After making the transfer, you can submit your 12-digit UTR reference right on the order page for quick verification.

What does 240 GSM mean?

GSM measures Grams per Square Meter. Conventional t-shirts range between 160–180 GSM, which can feel thin and limp. AXDORO tees use dense 240 GSM cotton that provides a structured, boxy drape, ensures zero transparency, and holds its silhouette over extended wear.

How long does delivery take?

Orders are dispatched within 24–48 working hours from our warehouse. Standard delivery takes 3–5 working days across major Indian metropolitan hubs, and 4–7 days for tier 2/3 locations. You will receive an AWB tracking number once shipped.

What is your return & exchange policy?

We provide a 7-day exchange window from the date of delivery for sizing adjustments. Items must be unwashed, unworn, and with tags intact. Please connect with our support desk on WhatsApp with your Order ID to initiate an exchange.

How can I track my shipment?

Visit our Track Order page and enter your Order ID and billing phone number to see live milestone updates.
